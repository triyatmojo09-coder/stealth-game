Bundled typefaces for Shadow Protocol — both are open-source fonts
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, subset to Latin characters
and converted to woff2 for this game. No network is ever used to load them.

- Black Ops One (blackops.woff2) — designed by James L. DeLaHunt / the
  Black Ops project (Sorkin Type). Display face used for titles and stamps.
- Rajdhani (rajdhani-medium.woff2, rajdhani-semibold.woff2,
  rajdhani-bold.woff2) — designed by Indian Type Foundry. HUD and body face.

Source: the google/fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts), which
distributes both families under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
